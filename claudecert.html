How to approach Anthropic's Claude courses & certification

Wonderful that you're curious about this. Here's the honest picture, checked against Anthropic's official pages (Oct 2026).

Two different things, often mixed up:

  • Claude Academy (free, open to everyone): short self-paced courses, each ending with a quiz and a shareable certificate of completion. academy.claude.com ↗
  • Claude Certification: a proctored exam with four tracks, compared below.

How every exam works: 120 minutes · multiple-choice and scenario-based questions · closed book · proctored by Pearson VUE ↗, online or at a test center · scored 100–1,000, 720 to pass · a Credly badge, valid 12 months · if it doesn't go your way, retake after 14, then 30, then 90 days (up to 4 tries in 12 months per exam). Prep is the free exam-prep courses in Anthropic Partner Academy.

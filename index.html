The six levels of IAM engineer, and where you fit.

Resume fixes

  1. Lead with a headline. 'Identity & Access Management Engineer | Entra ID, Conditional Access, PIM, Graph PowerShell | SC-300, Security+' instead of 'IT Support Coordinator'.
  2. Quantify JML. e.g. 'Processed ~X JML requests/month for 200+ users with Adaxes across AD, Entra ID and M365, with zero access left active after termination.' Use your brag file numbers.
  3. Explain 2010–2018 in one line. Add an honest one-line entry (family, relocation, other work, study). Recruiters mostly want to see that the gap is explained, not hidden.
  4. Rewrite the summary. 3 lines: years in identity work, the tools, the outcome you deliver (secure, audit-ready access), and the role you're targeting.
  5. Add Projects + GitHub. 'Entra JML automation (PowerShell + Graph)', 'Conditional Access baseline design', 'Okta–Entra SSO lab', with the GitHub link.
  6. Mirror keywords. Use the posting's exact words: IAM, IGA, RBAC, least privilege, JML, SSO, SAML, OIDC, SCIM, MFA, Conditional Access, PIM, access reviews, SailPoint, Okta, CyberArk, PowerShell, Microsoft Graph, Zero Trust, SOX.
  7. Certs on top. Security+ (SY0-701), AWS Cloud Practitioner, Microsoft Applied Skills (Entra; AD DS), SC-300 (in progress, exam date).

LinkedIn tips

  • Headline: 'IAM Engineer | Entra ID · Conditional Access · PIM · PowerShell/Graph | Security+ | SC-300 in progress'.

How would you design Conditional Access for a new tenant?

Report-only first, break-glass exclusions, MFA for all, block legacy auth, stronger controls for admins, device compliance, named locations, then monitor sign-in logs before enforcing.

What's the difference between SAML and OIDC?

SAML: XML assertions, mostly enterprise web SSO. OIDC: JSON/JWT ID tokens on top of OAuth 2.0, modern web and mobile. OAuth is authorization (access tokens), not authentication.

How do you enforce least privilege for admins?

PIM eligible roles, approval + MFA on activation, time-boxed, access reviews of role holders, separate admin accounts, no standing Global Admin.

Tell me about a time you found access that shouldn't exist.

STAR: shared-folder issue → compared Adaxes membership to the original Jira approval → manager sign-off → fixed and linked tickets for audit.

What is SCIM and why does it matter?

A standard API for automated provisioning and deprovisioning between an IdP and SaaS apps, so leavers lose access everywhere automatically.

How would you automate a leaver?

Side track

Side track: AI training work as a cybersecurity/IAM domain expert

AI labs pay domain experts to grade, correct and write rubrics for model answers. Your IAM and security background is exactly the kind of expertise they look for. It's flexible remote contract work you can do around your job, and it keeps you sharp on the newest AI.

What to learn

  • Data labeling basics. Classifying, tagging and correcting model outputs against written guidelines. Accuracy and following the guideline exactly matter more than speed.
  • RLHF and evaluation. Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback: you compare two answers, pick the better one and explain why. Evaluation means scoring answers for correctness, safety and helpfulness.
  • Rubric writing. Turning 'what a senior IAM engineer would expect' into clear, checkable criteria (e.g. 'mentions break-glass exclusion', 'never suggests disabling MFA'). Well-written rubrics are what top-paid experts get paid for.
  • Prompt grading and writing. Writing hard, realistic prompts (e.g. a tricky Conditional Access lockout scenario) and grading the model's answer step by step.
  • Your edge as a domain expert. Lead with IAM specifics: JML, Entra ID, Conditional Access, PIM, SAML/OIDC, AWS IAM, Security+. Experts with niche, verifiable experience get matched to the better-paid projects.
